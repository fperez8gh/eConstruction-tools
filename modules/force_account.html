Quick Guide
  • Add a Date: open Date ▾ and choose the date directly from the calendar.
  • A.1 Labor: enter Worker Classification, Worker Name, ST/OT/DT, and hours.
  • If Classification + Worker match the Contractor Rate Catalog, Rate and Fringe can populate from the catalog.
  • If there is no catalog match, enter the Rate and Fringe manually.
  • A.2 Fringe: worker, classification, and hours follow A.1; matching workers' hours are combined.
  • Enter Materials, Equipment, Subcontractor, and Work Accomplished as needed.
  • Data File: Save Data File to Excel and Open Data File to restore your sheets.
  • Print / Save PDF: use Print / Save PDF ▾ to print all dates or a specific date.
Rate Update Controls
🔒 Rates Locked: existing rates are protected.
🔓 Rates Unlocked: permits rate updates, but does not change rates by itself.
↻ Update Rates: explicitly applies the latest imported catalog to matching entries.
Field Colors
🔵 Light blue fields are linked or auto-filled.
⬜ White fields are available for manual entry.
